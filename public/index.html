    How to use this ⓘ
    1. Add or remove people. + Add person at the top of the list. To change or delete someone, tap their name — everything about them is in one place.
    2. Confirm people. Tap the status button on each person until the people you actually trust to turn up are green.
    3. Add flights. Tap a person and fill in Flying out and Flying home — even just the flight number helps. The Flights board groups everyone on the same flight, for planning the transfer to Zermatt.
    4. Assign beds. Use Choose bed on a person, or + Add on an empty bed. Set double rooms to Couple where two people share a bed.
    5. Check warnings before finalising. Red = a real problem (over capacity, someone in two beds). Amber = confirmed people still without a bed.
    6. Everyone shares this page. Send the link and anyone can confirm themselves or move beds — changes appear on everyone's screen within a few seconds, and the name shown at the top is who made the last one. Two people editing at once is fine; if someone takes the last bunk first, you'll be told rather than quietly overwritten.
    7. Keep a copy when it's settled. Print / PDF gives you a clean one-pager for the group chat.
