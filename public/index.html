    How it works
    1. Invite. Type a name under The group and press Invite. They start as Awaiting reply.
    2. Going or not. Tap any name to mark them Going, Awaiting reply or Not going, or to remove them. Not going frees their bed.
    3. Beds. Allocate beds fills the rooms automatically, or use Choose bed on a person, or + on an empty bed.
    4. Notes. Each person has a notes box for flights or anything else. It saves automatically.
    5. Shared. Everyone with the link sees the same plan, updated within a few seconds.
