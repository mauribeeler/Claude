    How to use this
    1. Add or remove people. Type a name under Add someone to the trip. To take someone off, use the red Delete button on their row.
    2. Confirm people. Tap the status pill on each person until the people you trust to turn up are green.
    3. Notes. Use the box under each name for flights, arrival times, anything else. It saves itself a moment after you stop typing — look for Saved ✓.
    4. Assign beds. Use Choose bed on a person, or + on an empty bed. Set double rooms to Couple where two people share a bed.
    5. Check warnings before finalising. Red is a real problem (over capacity, someone in two beds). Amber means confirmed people still without a bed.
    6. Everyone shares this page. Send the link and anyone can confirm themselves or move beds. Changes appear for everyone within a few seconds.
    7. Keep a copy when it's settled. Print / PDF gives you a clean one-pager for the group chat.
