    How to use this ⓘ
    1. Confirm people first. Tap the status button on each person until the people you actually trust to turn up are green.
    2. Assign rooms second. Use Assign on a person, or tap a bed slot to fill it. Set double rooms to Couple where two people are sharing a bed.
    3. Check warnings before finalising. Red = a real problem (over capacity, someone in two beds). Amber = confirmed people still without a bed.
    4. Export the final plan to share. Export JSON keeps the full plan; Print / PDF gives you a clean one-pager for the group chat.
