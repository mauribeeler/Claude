    How to use this ⓘ
    1. Confirm people first. Tap the status button on each person until the people you actually trust to turn up are green.
    2. Assign rooms second. Use Assign on a person, or tap a bed slot to fill it. Set double rooms to Couple where two people are sharing a bed.
    3. Check warnings before finalising. Red = a real problem (over capacity, someone in two beds). Amber = confirmed people still without a bed.
    4. Everyone shares this page. Send the link and anyone can confirm themselves or move beds — changes appear on everyone's screen within a few seconds, and the name shown at the top is who made the last one. Two people editing at once is fine; if someone takes the last bunk first, you'll be told rather than quietly overwritten.
    5. Keep a copy when it's settled. Export JSON saves the full plan as a file; Print / PDF gives you a clean one-pager for the group chat.
